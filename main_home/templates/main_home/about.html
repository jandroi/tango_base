About Tango

Giving the right number at the right time

Who we are

Tango is a data solutions boutique.

We focus on the structure underneath decisions — definitions, data flows, and rules — so numbers behave consistently across tools, teams, and time.

We don't sell dashboards, platforms, or generic analytics. We work where decisions start breaking because the underlying data no longer holds.

Why Tango exists

Across industries, the pattern is the same.

Numbers exist, reports are produced, and decisions are made — yet confidence is missing. Metrics change between meetings. Answers depend on who prepared them. Explanations arrive too late.

Over time, organizations compensate with workarounds: spreadsheets, manual checks, and personal logic. These solutions work — until they don't.

Tango exists to replace this fragility with structure.

How Tango thinks

We believe decision quality depends less on tools, and more on structure.

Reliable decisions require:

Clear definitions that are shared

Inputs that are known and traceable

Rules that exist once and are reused

When these elements are explicit, analytics becomes predictable.

When they are implicit, even good teams produce unstable results.

How Tango works with clients

Tango works closely with internal teams.

We don't replace existing systems unless necessary, and we don't impose frameworks for their own sake. Our role is to clarify, stabilize, and formalize what already exists — then help teams use it consistently.

Engagements are structured, scoped, and focused on concrete outcomes: fewer disputes, clearer answers, and decisions that don't need to be re-justified each cycle.
