About Tango

Tango is a boutique data consultancy focused on building reliable, structured data systems for organizations that need their numbers to work.

We work with teams facing the same challenge: data that exists but doesn't behave reliably across tools, teams, and reports. Whether it's financial services reconciling complex transactions, logistics operations managing procurement flows, or construction firms tracking project metrics-the pattern is the same.

Our approach is straightforward: align your data sources, build clean structure underneath, and deliver outputs you can trust. No over-engineering. No generic dashboards. Just the backbone your organization needs to make decisions with confidence.
