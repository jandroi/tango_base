What we build

What reliable numbers are made of

01

A map of your numbers

A single hierarchy that names every metric, dimension, and report your decisions depend on — and shows how they relate.

No more "is that revenue figure the same as the one in the board pack?" The map answers it.

02

Definitions everyone agrees on

A document where each metric has one written definition: what it includes, what it excludes, who owns it, and when it changes.

Definitions stop being things people carry in their heads. They become things you can read.

03

The rules layer

The logic that turns raw inputs into the numbers you report — written once, in one place, instead of recreated in every spreadsheet.

When a rule changes, it changes everywhere. When a number is questioned, the rule is there to point at.

04

A roadmap to get there

A prioritized sequence: what to fix first, what can wait, what to leave alone — designed so your team can execute it with us, with another partner, or on your own.

The engine

From source data to clean tables, every day.

Ingestion
Python that pulls data directly from your source-system APIs, or assembles the Excel files your teams already produce. The manual exports and copy-paste steps stop.
Storage
A data lake where all source data lands, untouched, in one place. One starting point for everything downstream.
Transformation
dbt models that turn raw inputs into clean staging tables, then into a consumption layer where the rules and definitions live as executable code.
Query layer
The final tables your PowerBI, dashboards, or machine-learning systems plug into directly. No more pulling extracts and reconciling.

The design defines what should be true. The engine makes it true every day, automatically.

Numbers that can carry decisions
